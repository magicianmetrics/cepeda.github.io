## About Me

I am an economist working at the Mexican Central Bank (Banco de México) in the Research Area. I have a BS at [Centro de Investigación y Docencia Económicas (CIDE)](https://www.cide.edu/de/){:target="_blank"} and I'm planning to start my graduate studies in 2026. Most of my research was done before starting at Banco de México and my work in no way represents the opinion of the central bank.

My research interests center on macroeconometrics, finance, causal inference and monetary policy. Despite this, while studying my bachelor degree I did some work on development.
